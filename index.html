ABOUT US

Celestial Atelier was born out of a deep passion for tactile artistry—bringing together the delicate precision of beaded jewelry and the cozy, intricate charm of crochet creations.

What started as a personal passion project quickly grew into a studio dedicated to making wearable art. Every piece in our collection—from sparkling seed-bead accessories to soft, hand-stitched crochet items—is crafted by hand with careful attention in detail, quality materials, and a touch of celestial wonder.
