# Celestial Atelier by Criselle

**Handmade Under the Stars** — An e-commerce web storefront showcasing handcrafted beaded jewelry and artisanal crochet accessories designed to bring a touch of cosmic magic to everyday style[cite: 1, 4].

---

## Overview

**Celestial Atelier by Criselle** is a responsive front-end e-commerce web application built for a handmade craft studio[cite: 1, 2]. It highlights artisanal craftsmanship, curated product collections, customer trust badges, and intuitive navigation wrapped in a dark luxury "Crimson Haze" aesthetic (deep burgundy, dusty rose, soft blush, and gold accents)[cite: 1, 2, 3, 4].

---

## Key Features

- **Navigation Header**:
  - Brand emblem logo with quick links to **Home**, **About Us**, **Products**, and **Contact Us**[cite: 1].
  - Integrated search bar, shopping cart counter badge, user profile icon, and mobile/drawer menu toggle[cite: 1].
- **Hero Section**:
  - Compelling brand introduction with **Sign in** and **Sign up** call-to-action buttons alongside a flat-lay showcase of handmade beaded pieces and yarn crafts[cite: 1].
- **About Us & Craftsmanship Pillars**:
  - Brand story paired with studio photography and three core value highlights[cite: 2]:
    - **100% Handcrafted** — Meticulously made by hand with zero mass production[cite: 2].
    - **Quality Materials** — Durable glass beads, premium yarn, and sturdy hardware[cite: 2].
    - **Thoughtfully Gifted** — Custom aesthetic packaging ready for gifting[cite: 2].
- **Interactive Product Showcase**:
  - Carousel slider featuring **Beaded Jewelry** (and additional collections) with an **Explore Collection** action button[cite: 3].
- **Trust & Service Banner**:
  - Highlights **Free Shipping** (on orders over ₱500), **Secure Payments** (100% secured checkout), and **Hassle-Free Returns** (7-day easy return policy)[cite: 3].
- **Comprehensive Footer**:
  - **Shop** directory (*All Products, Best Sellers, Beaded Accessories, Beaded Jewelry, Crochet Keychains, Crochet Wearables*).
  - **Company** links (*About Us, Our Materials, Sustainability, Customer Reviews, FAQs, Contact Us*)[cite: 4].
  - Social media and direct contact links (Facebook, Instagram, Email, Phone)[cite: 4].

---

## Built With

- **HTML5** — Semantic page structure (`index.html`)[cite: 1, 5]
- **CSS3** — Custom styling and responsive layouts (`style.css`)[cite: 5]
- **JavaScript** — Interactive carousel controls, navigation toggles, and cart state (`script.js`)[cite: 1, 3, 5]
